# Supplies Demand Check — Sep 12 2026 (S3, ~15 terms, Law 5)

Method: `web_search` (Brave) on GTA/generic queries for the 15 terms in the S3 brief. Purpose: steer titles + FAQ, not gate the build (pages ship regardless per Abram's direction).

## Headline (3 bullets)
- **Zero local (GTA) retailer competition on branded SKU searches.** "henry 630 adhesive", "henry 695", "ardex k15/p51/feather finish", "levelquik es" are all dominated by US big-box (Home Depot/Lowe's US), manufacturer TDS PDFs, Amazon, and US/cross-border distributors (tools4flooring, buildsite, chasephipps). Only Canadian hits: Lowe's.ca, HomeDepot.ca, and small pro-distributor sites (yourfloors.ca, thefloorbox.ca, ceramed.ca) — none of them Markham/GTA-local, none of them a full-service flooring retailer with a showroom. **A GTA flooring company with a branded-SKU supply page is close to a content vacuum for these exact-match queries.**
- **"toronto"-suffixed generic queries surface installer/service pages, not product pages.** "self leveling compound toronto", "vinyl plank adhesive toronto" return concrete-leveling contractors and installation-service pages (g1000home.ca, cngcontracting.com, tonysflooring.ca, squarefootflooring.com), not a retailer selling the actual product with a price + pickup. That's the wedge: our category pages (adhesives-primers, subfloor-prep) can rank for the toronto-generic tail AND the branded-SKU exact-match tail — nobody else is doing both.
- **"flooring supplies markham"** returns competitor flooring RETAILERS (Markham Flooring, Markham Custom Flooring, Oasis, Flooring Liquidators Markham) — not a supplies-specific hit. Confirms `/flooring-accessories` + category pages are genuinely new inventory for this exact local query, not something we're fighting an incumbent for.

## People-Also-Ask / recurring buyer questions (feed FAQ)
- Vinyl adhesive: "which Henry adhesive for which install method" (630 = standard pressure-sensitive/wet-set; 695 = high-RH/below-grade); coverage in sqft per pail is the #1 spec buyers look for.
- Self-leveler: "how thick can I pour in one shot" (LevelQuik ES: to 1.5"; Ardex K15: rapid-setting, tapered to elevations), "do I need a primer first" (yes — near-universal PAA across Ardex/Custom/Mapei self-levelers).
- Laminate/vinyl over concrete: "do I need a vapor/moisture barrier over concrete" — near-unanimous yes, "6-mil poly (or thicker) minimum" is the number that recurs across every independent source (Reddit DIY, aaflooring.net, wallyscarpet.com) — matches our Roberts Moisture Barricade 6-mil spec exactly.
- Floor vents: buyers search by exact size ("4x10") and material (wood species) far more than generic "floor vent" — confirms code-level detail pages (not just a category grid) are the right SEO unit for the floor_vent category; Six Points species-matched vents (Red Oak/White Oak/Maple) have a direct long-tail match nobody else GTA-local is serving.
- Adhesive coverage/"how much glue do I need": independent calculators exist (Mapei's own, calculatorconstruction.com) confirming buyers want a sqft→units calculator — validates the per-product inline calculator + the standalone `/flooring-accessories/calculator` page in this build.

## Steering decisions taken from this
- Product-page titles lead with the exact brand+model string ("Henry 630 PeachPro", "Ardex K 15") — that's the literal top-ranking query pattern, not a generic "vinyl adhesive" title.
- Category page FAQ leans on the recurring PAA above (install-method matching, primer-first, 6-mil minimum, one-pour thickness) rather than invented questions.
- Floor vent codes get individual product pages (not folded into a single "floor vents" grid card) — search behavior is size+species specific.
